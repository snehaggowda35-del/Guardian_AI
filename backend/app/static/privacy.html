GUARDIAN AI

Privacy and safety

Guardian AI is parent-authorized safety monitoring. It collects only text explicitly enabled by the parent, uses it to detect safety signals, and discloses only the triggering text and limited relevant context in an alert.

What we retain

Normal events are minimized and discarded. Safety-relevant events are retained for the account so the parent can review them. Parents can request deletion and should define a retention period before production launch.

Who can see it

Only the authenticated parent account that owns a linked device can view alerts. Transport must be HTTPS in production. We do not sell data, serve advertising, or read unrelated content.

Important limits

Results are automated signals, not diagnoses or emergency services. A parent remains responsible for deciding how to respond.
